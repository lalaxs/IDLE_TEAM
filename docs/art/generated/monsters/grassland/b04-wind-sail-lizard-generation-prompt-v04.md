# B04 风帆蜥生成提示词 v04

## 修订目标

弃用风耳兽形象，改为更符合风语栈桥生态、且具有完整 Boss 精度的双背帆蜥类生物。

## 最终提示词

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: original Boss concept art for the mobile H5 idle RPG “青丘远征”

Create one completely original fantasy sail-backed lizard Boss named 风帆蜥 for
stage 1-4 “风语栈桥”:
- low, broad and heavy four-legged lizard with a wedge-shaped head
- exactly four short powerful legs with broad rounded gripping claws
- exactly two tall leaf-shaped dorsal sails sweeping backward
- each sail has a thick ochre rim, gray-green membrane and one dark support ridge
- one long thick tail curling low to the side with a blunt tip
- exactly two amber oval eyes in dark eye sockets
- one simple closed jaw line with no teeth
- five to seven large natural scale plates across shoulders, flanks and upper legs
- heavy forward shoulders and a grounded Boss stance

Use compact hand-drawn sticker-like game art, extra-thick warm near-black outlines,
medium-thick internal lines, flat color blocks and one hard-edged shadow per major
form. Match the structural detail of the other grassland Bosses without using
micro-detail.

Palette: muted olive green, warm ochre, desaturated gray-green, earthy brown,
amber eyes and warm near-black outlines.

Show one full-body creature in front three-quarter view facing right. Keep both
sails, all four legs, head, claws and complete tail visible. Center it with generous
padding on a perfectly flat solid #ff00ff chroma-key background.

Avoid wings, feathers, fur, ears, horns, metal armor, harness, clothing, weapons,
jewelry, mechanical parts, wood construction, tiny scales, excessive spikes,
open mouth, teeth, wind effects, bridge props, text, logo, watermark, scenery,
floor, shadow, realistic anatomy, painterly lighting and complex gradients.
```
